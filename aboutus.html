About Gateway Education Services

Discover our story, mission, and values that shape the future of education in Uganda

Our Story

In 2011 Gateway opened its doors and launched its journey to bring education to the people of Uganda. The passion for keeping the memory of why we started alive, mixed with the constant innovation and search for new perspective have been and will continue to be the main ingredients in a recipe bound for success.

Welcome to Gateway Education Services, a Christian-founded institution nestled in the heart of Bwerenga, along Entebbe Road. Our journey began with a vision, a vision that transcends conventional education. We believe in holistic education, where not only academic prowess but also spiritual and character development are paramount.

At Gateway Education Services, we aspire to nurture individuals who not only excel academically but also possess the moral and ethical foundation to make a positive impact on any good society. Our commitment extends beyond the confines of Uganda, as we strive to prepare our students to thrive on a global scale.

Our legacy of academic excellence is reflected in our consistent track record of impressive academic results. However, what truly sets us apart is our dedication to shaping well-rounded individuals, and instilling in them the values of integrity, compassion, and a quest for excellence.

Empowering Innovation, Inspiring Minds

At Gateway, we believe that imagination and creativity are the keys to unlocking a brighter future. Our mission is to inspire minds and empower innovation, driving progress and positive change in the world around us.

At Gateway, we are committed to a three-fold path of supportive education designed to give our students the confidence to take on new challenges and achieve their full potential. We provide each child with a stimulating and supportive learning environment which celebrates their uniqueness and capabilities.

We value thought-provoking teaching practices, the latest curriculum and wide-ranging extra-curricular activities that will inspire each child to utilize spaces both inside and outside the classroom and develop skills that will last for life.

Every child is guided in ethical and social behavior in every detail or activity on the daily and annual school calendar. We strive above all to create socially responsible citizens for the Uganda of tomorrow.

Encouraging the Child's Holistic Development

Students participate in a strong co-curricular programme which includes athletics, visual arts and performing arts such as music and drama. As a result, students are able to develop a broad engagement with the world as we seek to educate the "whole child".

Students are also encouraged to develop ethically and spiritually and to strive to succeed. Gateway Education Services ethos nurtures principled individuals who display respect towards diverse cultures and beliefs, enabling them to become responsible, compassionate global citizens.

Facilities include a well-equipped library with an audio visual centre, an ICT room with SMART-Boards, science laboratories, sports fields, art-rooms and a common room and lounge for the IB students.

Our Mission

Empowering students to become curious, creative, and critical thinkers, preparing them for a lifetime of learning and success.

Igniting a passion for knowledge, fostering a love of learning, and inspiring students to reach their full potential.

Our Vision

To inspire a love of learning that extends beyond the classroom, fostering a culture of innovation, creativity, and critical thinking.

To empower students to become confident, compassionate, and engaged global citizens, prepared to make a positive difference in the world.

Extra-Curricular Activities

Sports at Gateway

We offer a wide range of sports, artistic and creative activities and career mentorship to help develop our students' talents and interests. We pay tribute to our teachers who have the qualifications and experience to reach and maintain the academic expectations that we set for ourselves.

We hold an annual cultural day featuring music, dance and drama inter-house competitions. Games and sports include football, rugby, basketball, volleyball, you name it, we've got it.

Our dedicated staff members, along with Alumni groups work together to give students more flexibility in accessing various programs and courses that we offer.

Music, Dance and Drama

We hold an annual cultural day featuring music, dance and drama inter-house competitions. Games and sports include football, rugby, basketball, volleyball, you name it, we've got it.

Our cultural dance troupe performs traditional dances from various tribes, promoting cultural awareness and appreciation.

Students can learn folk dances, ballet, hip-hop, and contemporary styles, developing their coordination and rhythm. Guest artists and workshops introduce students to new styles and techniques.
